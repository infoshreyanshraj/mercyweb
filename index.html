About the project

AI that feels like yours.

MERCY is an AI app project focused on making AI conversations feel clear, personal, and easy to use. The current direction combines an elegant purple-and-black visual identity, a glowing central visual, and a modern Liquid Glass interface.

The planned app is being built with Flutter and Dart, with Android APK and Windows EXE as target deliverables. Provider/model selection and user-supplied API-key configuration are part of the intended experience.
